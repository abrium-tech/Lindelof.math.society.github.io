# Upcoming Activities

### 1. Foundations of Pure Mathematics Seminars
*   **Focus:** Abstract Analysis, Topology, and Group Theory.
*   **Format:** Weekly Reading Group. Members take turns presenting proofs and dissecting theorems from  mathematical texts.

### 2. The History & Impact of Science
*   **Focus:** The intersection of scientific temperament, society, and history. 
*   **Format:** Monthly Discussion Forum. We review the mathematical legacy, philosophies, and societal impact of monumental figures (such as the life and work of Abdus Salam).

### 3. Problem of the Week
*   **Focus:** Collaborative problem solving.
*   **Format:** An intensely challenging proof is posted on our notice board every Monday. We gather on Fridays to discuss the most elegant solutions.
