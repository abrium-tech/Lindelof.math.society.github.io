# About the Lindelöf Math Society

Located at the UM–DAE Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences, our society is a community driven by a deep, inherent passion for pure mathematics. 

Our primary objective is to explore the different domains of mathematical sciences. We approach mathematics not merely as a practical, computational tool for other disciplines, but as a profound and beautiful subject worthy of dedicated theoretical study. 

We foster an environment of intense discussion, rigorous proof, and shared curiosity for anyone who wants to push the boundaries of their mathematical understanding.
